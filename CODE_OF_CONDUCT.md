# Code of conduct

## The short version

Be decent to people. Assume good faith. Disagree about code, not about each other.

## What we expect

Welcoming and patient behaviour toward people of any background or skill level.
Criticism aimed at the work, offered with a reason and a suggested alternative.
Graceful acceptance when a decision does not go your way.

## What is not acceptable

Harassment, personal attacks, or demeaning comments. Sexualised language or imagery.
Publishing anyone's private information without permission. Sustained disruption of
discussion. Deliberately making someone feel unwelcome.

## Scope

This applies in the issue tracker, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where
you are representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report problems to the maintainers through private vulnerability reporting or by opening
an issue asking for a private contact. Reports are handled confidentially. Maintainers
may edit or remove contributions, and may block accounts, when this document is breached.
